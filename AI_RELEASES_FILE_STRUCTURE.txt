API ENDPOINT SUMMARY
====================

GET /api/verticals/ai-releases/health
├── Response: {"status": "ok", "service": "ai-releases-predictor"}

POST /api/verticals/ai-releases/predict
├── Request: {provider, model_name, target_date}
├── Response: {predicted_probability, polymarket_price, edge, recommendation, confidence}

POST /api/verticals/ai-releases/batch-predict
├── Request: {predictions: [{provider, model_name, target_date}, ...]}
├── Response: {predictions: [...], total_edge_usd}

POST /api/verticals/ai-releases/markets
├── Request: {provider, model_name, query}
├── Response: {markets, top_market, current_price}

GET /api/verticals/ai-releases/examples
├── Response: {examples: [...], generated_at}

GET /api/verticals/ai-releases/leaderboard
├── Query: ?min_edge=0.05
├── Response: {leaderboard: [...], count, total_edge_usd}

GET /api/verticals/ai-releases/providers
├── Response: {providers: [{name, display}, ...]}
